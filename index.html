Penguins and Pears

A tiny Transformer, written from scratch, that generates Russian internet rants about pears.
It runs entirely in this tab; nothing is sent anywhere.

Loading...

The training data began with 82 culture-war posts by a Russian online troll. Each post was rewritten one for one. Whatever the author despised became pears. Whatever he held up as good became penguins. Each rewrite kept the original's rhythm, length, paragraph breaks and emotional temperature, right down to the ALL-CAPS bursts, rhetorical questions, mock expertise, P.S. endings, abrupt verdicts and profanity.

Grievance rhetoric can run on almost any subject. A rant about pears can follow the same pattern as a rant about immigrants. You still get the us-versus-them framing, the show of reasoning, the pose of someone who's seen it all before, and a verdict handed down without evidence. Change the target and the voice is instantly recognizable.

It's also a joke. Somebody had to get very angry about pears.

How much of that pattern can a small language model learn from scratch? If the cadence carries across subjects, 82 rewrites and variations on them might be enough for a model with about 2.4 million parameters to pick it up.

The 82 source posts are available here only as pear-and-penguin rewrites.

2,403,840 parameters. Thanks to the unnamed Russian edgelord who inspired the whole thing.
